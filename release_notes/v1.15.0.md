# Release Notes v1.15.0

This release lets maps and areas be mowed with a new angle for every job: random or turning by a step. Their route is no longer saved but calculated whenever a job is put together. Calculating and loading routes shows one progress bar until the new route is really shown.

## Random and dynamic angle

- New setting **Angle mode** in the mow settings, next to the angle: **Fixed** (as before), **Random (at least 25° change)** and **Dynamic (turns by a step)** with **Start angle** and **Angle step per job** (default 25°, so that the angle does not land on 90° every few jobs). Random picks a new angle at least 25° away from the last one (Squares: within their 90° repeat). Dynamic starts with the start angle and turns by the step with every calculation. The pattern **Rings** has no angle; the setting is hidden there and ignored.
- Maps and areas with a random or dynamic angle (**dynamic areas**) have no saved route: their waypoints are not saved, not kept in map drafts and not exported, and they are never marked **⚠ recalculate**. The route is calculated for every job instead. The angle last sent to the mower is remembered per map or area, and the next route continues from it. Changing the start angle or the angle mode starts again at the start angle.
- Map editor: **Edit waypoints** is not offered for dynamic areas, and **Calculate** becomes **Sim calculate**. It calculates an example route with the next angle, which is shown in the editor and can be uploaded as it is, but is not saved and does not mark the map unsaved. Loading a dynamic area in the editor calculates nothing; its route stays hidden until **Sim calculate**.
- Main dashboard: **Areas** puts dynamic areas together with new angles; a single dynamic area is loaded and calculated right away. While a dynamic area is shown, the new button **Recalculate** calculates the route again with the next angles, and **Upload** sends the route shown. Their selections are highlighted on the map in the colour of their part of the job, with their number, as in the scheduler.
- Scheduler: **Show route** only shows an example; a scheduled job calculates its dynamic areas again when it is uploaded. Putting a job together can take longer now; the preview waits up to 90 s for the route.

## Progress

- Calculating a route shows one progress bar: the calculation up to 80 %, the transfer of the new route to the browser up to 100 %. Before, a second, transparent bar for loading the map appeared on the map.
- The bar stays until the new route is shown, also when a slow WLAN delays the transfer. Putting a job together shows **Loading route** until the whole job route has arrived; before, the old route stayed on the map for a while without any notice.

## Map

- Switching the **Mow areas** on the main dashboard no longer marks a saved map as unsaved, and the **Updating…** hint appears next to the compass instead of changing the height of the tile.
- Changing the selection of an area no longer sends all waypoints to the modem again. After a calculation the message was often larger than 64 KB and was dropped, so the next calculation used the old selection; **Calculate** now also waits until the modem has the latest selection.
- The coloured areas in the scheduler preview no longer cover the whole map with the number in its centre while their selections are still loading, and they are requested again when the answer gets lost.
- The export dialog lists the areas of a map as a tree again; sub-areas were indented below the wrong area.
