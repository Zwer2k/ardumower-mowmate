# Release Notes v1.14.0

This release adds areas: parts of a map with their own selection, mow settings and route. Several areas can be combined into one mowing job, started right away from the main dashboard or by the scheduler. Jobs that do not fit into the mower's memory are split into parts that run one after another.

## Areas

- A map can now have **areas** (sub-maps). An area uses the perimeter, exclusions and docking points of its map and adds its own **selection**, its own **mow settings** and its own **route**. Areas can have areas of their own, to group them; every area mows its own selection, limited only by the perimeter and the exclusions, not by the selection of the area above. A new area starts with the selection, route and mow settings of the map or area it is created under.
- New button **Area** under **Manage maps** creates an area of the loaded map. The map must be saved first, because the area uses the saved geometry. 
- The map dropdown shows the maps as a tree, with their areas indented below them. While an area is loaded, the geometry of its map is shown greyed out and cannot be edited, and the badge **Area of …** names the map it belongs to.
- The selection is drawn in the toolbar **Calculate waypoints**: **Select a rectangle**, **Select with lasso** (draw the outline freehand) and **Select the whole map**. Its points can be moved in the map editor under **Edit selection**, with undo and redo.
- Calculating the route of an area mows only what lies inside the perimeter and inside the selection, around the exclusions. All patterns, the route order and **Mow exclusion borders while passing** work as for a map.
- New option **Border laps along the selection edge** in the mow settings of an area. Off (default): the area is mowed right up to the selection edge, and the border laps only run where the selection meets the real perimeter. On: the selection edge is treated like the perimeter, with **Distance to border** and border laps.
- An area whose route no longer fits is marked **⚠ recalculate** in the map dropdown and **⚠ Route outdated** next to its badge. This happens when the geometry of its map is changed and saved, or when its selection changes. Calculate the route of the area again.
- Deleting a map also deletes its areas; the confirmation lists them.

## Mowing jobs

- New button **Areas** on the main dashboard. Choose a map (every saved map, with or without areas) and tick the whole map and/or its areas, put them in order with ▲/▼ and press **Show on map**. The modem puts the routes of the areas together and connects them with drives around the exclusions. The main dashboard and the live map then show the job as a preview, every area in its own colour, marked **Vorschau … · nicht hochgeladen**. **Upload** sends it to the mower, **Start** mows it. **Show the loaded map again** in the dialog drops the preview. A single map or area (**Load map**) is not turned into a job: it is loaded as the current map, and its **Mow areas** switches, Upload and Start work as usual.
- Each area keeps its own **Mow areas** switches (Area, Border, Excl. border) in the job. While a job is shown, the **Mow areas** switches on the main dashboard belong to the job: what is switched off there is left out in all of its areas. Switching puts the job together again as a preview; upload it again before starting. Scheduled jobs always run with all parts.
- New option **Mow overlapping parts only once**, shown when two or more areas are selected. Off (default): every area is mowed completely, overlaps twice. On: tracks of an area that lie in an earlier area of the job are cut off at its edge and left out; the remaining pieces are connected by drives around the exclusions. Nothing is recalculated.
- Jobs use the saved versions of the areas; unsaved changes are not included. Areas with an outdated route cannot be selected.
- While a job is on the mower, the main dashboard and the live map show the job's route instead of the route of the loaded map: every area in its own colour, mowed tracks greyed out. The status reads **Auftrag: …** with the names of the areas, and the waypoint counter counts through the whole job. Start, Stop, Dock and Skip stay enabled.

## Large jobs

- Before uploading a job, the modem estimates from the mower's free memory how many points fit, the same way as the warning before a map upload. A larger job is split into parts, at the border between two areas where possible.
- The parts run one after another: the mower mows a part, Sunray sends it to the docking station at the end (or it stops there without a station), and once the battery is charged (charging current at or below 0.2 A for two minutes, at the latest after five hours) the modem uploads the next part and starts it. The status shows **Teil 2/3** and so on.
- The series stops when the mower is stopped or reports an error in the middle of a part, when another map is uploaded, or when a new job is started. Docking to charge in the middle of a part is part of Sunray's normal operation and does not stop it.
- The series is kept in the modem's memory only and is lost when the modem restarts.

## Scheduler

- A schedule entry now mows a list of areas in a fixed order, with the option **Mow overlapping parts only once**, chosen with the same map list and tree as **Areas** on the main dashboard. The list shows the names of all areas of an entry.
- The scheduler is now a page of its own, **Schedule** in the main menu, instead of a panel of the map editor. The list and the editor of the selected entry are on the left (above the map on narrow screens), the map of the entry fills the rest. It shows the areas of the job in colour with their number in the job; **Show route** in its top left corner lets the modem put the job together and draws its route (this replaces a preview on the main dashboard), **Show areas** goes back. The entry's map does not have to be loaded.
- A new entry starts without a map: choose the map in the editor. A map without areas is ticked as a whole right away.
- Existing entries keep working: they become a list with their previous map.
- The scheduler starts its entries as jobs: large maps and jobs are split into parts here as well. An entry is skipped while the mower is mowing or a job is still running in parts, also while the mower charges between two parts.
- Renaming an area updates the entries that use it. When a map or area is deleted, the entries that use it are switched off, not removed.

## Export and import

- Exporting a map in the **Grauonline JSON** format can include its areas: tick the areas under **Include areas**. Selecting an area also selects the areas above it; deselecting one also deselects the areas below it.
- Areas are exported with their selection and mow settings, not with their routes. Importing a file with areas offers them under **Import areas**. The modem then saves the imported map at once, because areas need a saved map, creates the areas below it and marks them **⚠ recalculate**.
- Files without areas import as before. CaSSAndRA GeoJSON carries no areas.

## User interface

- The main menu marks **Map** and **Schedule** with a yellow dot while they have unsaved changes (an edited map or a map draft, an unsaved schedule).
- On narrow phones the menu buttons share the width of the header, so the menu on the right is no longer cut off; their icons are centred.
- The dropdowns **Map** and **Repeat** in the scheduler and in **Choose areas** open on top of the page and are no longer cut off by the scrolling editor. They close when they are scrolled out of the editor.
