# Release Notes v1.8.0

This release fixes firmware uploads that crawled, failed or turned red although they succeeded, gets modems with a weak on-board antenna onto WiFi, and makes WiFi faster while Bluetooth is on. Saving a map now shows its progress, all progress appears in one overlay, the status dashboard is redesigned, and the settings show how much storage and memory is in use.

## Firmware Upload

- An aborted upload no longer leaks memory. Each aborted modem upload kept its 3 MB buffer, so after two aborts no further upload got one (measured: 1.8 of 8 MB PSRAM free). Aborted uploads now release the buffer, and mower upload sessions, which were never released, are released too.
- The progress no longer turns red while the modem flashes. On the ESP32-S3 the firmware is flashed after the upload, and flashing plus restart often take longer than the 30 seconds the browser waited. It now waits up to 5 minutes and turns green as soon as the modem is back.

## WiFi

- Modems that loop on `Reason: 2 - AUTH_EXPIRE` and get no IP address connect within seconds. Some ESP32-S3 boards transmit distorted at full power although the signal is good. After three failed logins at a good signal the modem lowers its transmit power step by step (19.5, 15, 11, 8.5 dBm) and remembers the level that worked. Before, such a modem needed a minute or more, or did not connect at all.
- Failed logins at a weak signal, for example when the mower is far from the access point, no longer lower the power. If even 8.5 dBm keeps failing, the modem goes back to full power instead of staying low.
- The modem retries on its own when a login attempt fails and no new attempt follows within 5 seconds. Before, it could wait 30 seconds for the WiFi health check.
- New setting **Transmit power** for WiFi client mode: Automatic (default) or a fixed 19.5, 15, 11 or 8.5 dBm. A board with a poorly matched on-board antenna can transmit cleaner with less power: one test board reached the router at only 5.5 Mbps with 44.8 % retries from 3 m away. Lower power also shortens the range, so boards with an external antenna should stay on Automatic.
- The log shows the transmit power and the signal strength of failed logins.

## Bluetooth

- Bluetooth leaves WiFi much more airtime. It advertised every 3.75 to 11.25 ms and kept connections at 2.5 to 12.5 ms, both below what the Bluetooth standard allows, and occupied the shared radio almost all the time, even without a connected app. It now advertises every 500 to 1000 ms and connects at 30 to 60 ms. The app needs 1 to 2 seconds to find the modem.
- The Bluetooth settings explain that Bluetooth slows down WiFi: while Bluetooth is on, the modem has to keep WiFi in power-save mode. Measured with Bluetooth on before this release: about 190 ms response time and 6 to 9 KB/s, compared with 30 ms and 300 to 570 KB/s with Bluetooth off.

## Map

- Saving a map shows its progress in percent, from preparing the data to writing the file. Before, saving gave no feedback.
- Saving and calculating show a progress indicator right after the click. Before, it only appeared once the modem started, after the edited map had been transferred to it.
- Switching a mow area toggle no longer transfers the whole map to the browser again. The toggle only changes the mow settings, but the modem marked the map as changed and resent all waypoints with the loading overlay.
- Each status message to the browser copied the whole map, thousands of waypoints, only to read the dock position. It now reads just the dock points, which speeds up status updates and progress reports.

## User Interface

- The status dashboard has the style of the main dashboard: a summary strip with colored status tags and cards for mower, battery, position & GPS, mowing statistics, faults & recoveries, mower controller, modem and sensors. Durations show as hours and minutes, a bar shows how much of the mowing time had a GPS fix, and the new modem card shows firmware, uptime, last reset, WiFi signal, channel, IP, transmit power and memory use.
- All progress uses one overlay. The green bar under the header is gone: calculating, saving, clearing and uploading to the mower show the same panel as loading a map.
- The dashboard mini map shows the map loading progress, like the editor.
- A new **Storage** group in the settings shows the use of the flash storage for maps and settings, of internal RAM (including the lowest free value since start) and of PSRAM, refreshed on demand.
- Toggle and selection settings show their help texts. The mower settings already had texts that were never displayed.
- The heartbeat log line is no longer cut off. It shows only the reset reason; the details stay in the lines logged at boot.

## Upgrade Notes

- If a modem loses large packets or stalls during uploads although the signal is good, set **Transmit power** to 11 or 8.5 dBm, or use a board with an external antenna. The router's receive rate for the modem shows which setting works best.
- If an upload from the web interface keeps failing on a modem with a poor connection, install this release over USB.
- Saving the settings restarts the modem, as before.
