# Release Notes v1.16.1

This release fixes the laps around the exclusions and a map transfer that never finished for areas with a detailed selection, lets the distances to the border and the exclusions be set in half track widths, keeps the routes of areas with a random or dynamic angle when only the Mow areas are switched, and ends the progress bar as soon as a new route is shown.

## Mow settings

- **Distance to border** and **Distance to exclusions** can now be set in steps of half a track width (0, 0.5, 1, 1.5 …). The route planner used to cut the distance to the border down to a whole number and rounded the distance to the exclusions, so a half step had no effect.

## Route

- The laps around the exclusions no longer get small hooks at their corners or open up into a detour across the area. The laps lie exactly on the exclusion edge, but the geometry library rounds their corners to 1 mm, so a corner could end up a fraction of a millimetre inside the exclusion. The final safety check then took the edge between two such corners for a drive through the exclusion and rerouted it around the corners; in a test map this hit 58 of 210 lap edges. Ends within 2 mm of an edge now count as on the edge, as points on the boundary already did.

## Map transfer

- An area with a detailed selection (drawn with the lasso, more than about 140 points) no longer keeps the map transfer to the browser from ever finishing. The modem attached the whole selection to every part of the transfer; with that many points it filled the 4 KB of a part on its own, no map point fitted in any more, and the modem sent the same empty part over and over. The browser showed "Loading map" with changing texts forever, and no other message from the modem got through. The selection now goes out once per transfer, rounded to centimetres, and every part carries at least one map point.

## Main dashboard

- New switch in **Choose areas**: **Mark areas with a random or dynamic angle on the map**. Off hides the coloured areas with their number on the main dashboard map. The browser remembers the choice.
- Switching the **Mow areas** while a job is shown no longer calculates its areas with a random or dynamic angle again. The modem keeps their routes and angles from the last time the job was put together and only filters them anew, so switching is quick and the route stays the same. **Recalculate**, **Areas** and **Show route** in the scheduler still calculate new routes; a changed area is also calculated again.

## Progress

- Calculating a route no longer stays at 80 % for about 30 seconds after the new route is already shown. The modem holds back other messages while it transfers the map, so its "complete" message arrives after the transfer; the bar waited for a transfer that had already finished.
