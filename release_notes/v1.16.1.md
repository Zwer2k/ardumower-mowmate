# Release Notes v1.16.1

This release fixes the laps around the exclusions, lets the distances to the border and the exclusions be set in half track widths, and ends the progress bar as soon as a new route is shown.

## Mow settings

- **Distance to border** and **Distance to exclusions** can now be set in steps of half a track width (0, 0.5, 1, 1.5 …). The route planner used to cut the distance to the border down to a whole number and rounded the distance to the exclusions, so a half step had no effect.

## Route

- The laps around the exclusions no longer get small hooks at their corners or open up into a detour across the area. The laps lie exactly on the exclusion edge, but the geometry library rounds their corners to 1 mm, so a corner could end up a fraction of a millimetre inside the exclusion. The final safety check then took the edge between two such corners for a drive through the exclusion and rerouted it around the corners; in a test map this hit 58 of 210 lap edges. Ends within 2 mm of an edge now count as on the edge, as points on the boundary already did.

## Progress

- Calculating a route no longer stays at 80 % for about 30 seconds after the new route is already shown. The modem holds back other messages while it transfers the map, so its "complete" message arrives after the transfer; the bar waited for a transfer that had already finished.
