# Release Notes v1.12.0

This release shows the mowing progress on the maps like CaSSAndRA, can draw the tracks as wide as the mower cuts, completes the terminal's command list and makes the motor display work with Sunray versions without motor telemetry, such as upstream Sunray.

## Maps

- The main dashboard map and the live map show the mowing progress like CaSSAndRA: tracks still to mow in green, mowed tracks faded grey and the stretch to the next waypoint dashed. All lines keep their width when zooming. The live map showed the route as one thin grey line before, without progress.
- New setting **Cut width** (Settings → Mower). With **Tracks: cut width** under the Mow areas switches, the map editor draws the tracks as wide as the mower cuts, under the route, so overlaps and gaps between neighbouring tracks become visible.

## Main Dashboard

- The motor tile (rpm and current of the mow and drive motors) needs a Sunray that answers AT+S5. Without it, such as upstream Sunray, the tile showed 0 rpm and 0.00 A as if the motors were standing. It is now hidden, together with its chart.
- When Sunray does not answer AT+S5 three times while answering its status, the modem stops asking and checks again every 10 minutes; one answer is enough to treat it as supported for good. Unanswered requests held up the connection to the mower every 5 seconds before.

## Terminal

- The list of known commands (right click or Ctrl+→ in the command field) covers the AT commands of Sunray, grouped into queries, control, diagnostics, restart and Linux, and marks the commands that only the MowMate Sunray fork answers. Three entries were wrong: **AT+W,50** and **AT+S2,30** do not set the mowing progress or the cutter height (that is AT+C), and the u-blox commands are sent as **AT+UBX**, not AT+U.
- AT commands typed into the terminal get their checksum appended. Sunray removes the last field of a console command as its checksum even without checking it, so **AT+M,0.1,0** arrived as AT+M,0.1. A command that already ends with a checksum is sent unchanged.
- The command field shows a hint with an example, how to open the list of known commands and that the checksum is added.
